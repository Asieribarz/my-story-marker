There are two ways off a step like that. One is the punt, with the mechanism in the crate and the lens left where it is, and a lamp that cannot be made again inside a month. The other is down.

Mara had known the answer since the bell. What took the time was the arithmetic her body was doing without being asked: forty feet of green water off the edge of the reef, the old quarry channel under it, no bottom she would be able to see, nothing for the eye to stand on.

She took her coat off and gave it to her brother, because a coat two sizes large will drown you.

"Mara."

"Third step," she said. "Ringbolt. You said the lashing is three turns and a hitch?"

"Three and a half. Mara, I will go, it is my - "

"You cannot swim and I can. Hold the crate."

She did not give herself the second she would have used to think better of it. She went off the second step feet first with the knife in her fist, and the water took her in the way that water takes you in the first week of a storm season, which is not like cold but like being held.

Down. The green went out of it at a fathom and after that there was only the dark and the reef going past her hands, step, step, step, and the sound of her own heart arriving at somewhere behind her ears, and no bottom, and no end to it, and every one of the nineteen years she had spent refusing to do this lined up to tell her to turn round.

She did not turn round.

The ringbolt was where he said. The lashing was three turns and a half and the knife went through it on the second draw, and the lens came off the reef into her arms, dead-heavy and as awkward as a water butt, liftable at all only because the water carried half of it, and she went up the way you go up when there is no more air, which is without any dignity at all.

She came out on the second step on her knees with the lens in both arms and her brother's hands under her elbows, and she got one breath, and then another, and she was still holding it.

Out north, across two miles of black water, the bell had stopped ringing, and it did not start again.
