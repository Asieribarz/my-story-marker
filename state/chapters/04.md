Dov Ashe ran Mara down on the flats and did not take the page. He named the ringer: the warden herself, on the first, at dusk, three strokes for Vell - because a keeper entered dead is a keeper the roll can be closed on, and a light with no keeper passes to the nearest warden. The name over her brother's on the roll was Ashe's own. He lent her the warden's punt and broke with his orders doing it. She found Tem alive on the second step of the Drowned Stair, the mechanism crated in oakum and the lens lashed to a ringbolt under the third step, and got the account: a written direction under article eleven to show the Cold Lamp at four flashes to the minute, which is the character of the Sound light eleven miles south over clean water; one vessel onto the reef on the second and nine men taken off her; and his answer, which was to strip the light rather than show it lying. Two more had come on in the dark since, and three men with them, and he knew the number. He would not leave without the mechanism. Then the Calder bell rang twice for a wreck, and Mara gave him her coat, went off the step with a knife into forty feet of green water, and brought the lens up. When she surfaced the bell had stopped.
