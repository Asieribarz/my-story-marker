id: s3
name: Gannet Isle
---

**Appearance.** Low, wide and treeless, with a salvage yard behind a wall of wreck timber
and a warden's house at the top of it. Everything on the island has been something else
first: the gate posts are masts, the yard wall is decking, the roofs are hatch covers.

**Atmosphere.** Prosperous in a way nowhere else in the Banks is, and nobody on it says out
loud why.

**Function.** Where the wreck law lives, and where the story learns what a dark light is
worth in casks and coin.
