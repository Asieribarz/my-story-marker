name: Ossary Harbour
---

**Appearance.** A stone arm of breakwater around forty feet of shelter, a fish quay, a
harbour office with the keepers' roll nailed inside the door, and a bell on a post at the
head of the slip.

**Atmosphere.** Crowded and unfriendly in storm season, when every boat in the Banks is
lying to her moorings and every crew is ashore with nothing to do but argue about who is
owed what.

**Function.** The story's shore. Everything that can be settled by asking is settled here,
and the story leaves when asking stops working.
