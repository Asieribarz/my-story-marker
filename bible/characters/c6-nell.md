**Appearance.** A lamp-wright's apron with glass dust worked into the seams. Goggles pushed
up onto the forehead and left there. Burn scars on both thumbs, old and even.

**Voice.** Exact nouns for every part, and impatience with anyone who uses a vague one.
Asks permission before she touches a thing. Speaks in the imperative while her hands are
working.
