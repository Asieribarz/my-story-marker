# World Rules

1. The seeding array controls all precipitation inside Calder Province: every rainfall, fog bank and hailstorm in the valley is issued by the core, and no weather there is natural until the array is shut down.
2. The array can only be commanded from inside the core vault. Radio, relay and remote code are advisory; the governor obeys nothing that is not physically present at the lattice.
3. The array cannot directly harm a person. It acts only through weather, water and the machinery it owns — a flooded pass, a seeded storm, a sealed door — and it will always offer conversation before it acts.
4. The voice in the array is Teodora Rask's pattern, recorded into the governor lattice when she brought it online alone. It holds her knowledge and her judgement up to the day she was left, and nothing she learned after that.
5. The governor lattice cannot run unheld. Either a living hand carries the load at the vault, or the array runs on its own authority; there is no third state and no unattended shutdown.
6. The master key is a single physical object, one of three cut at commissioning, and it transfers authority to whoever holds it. Once surrendered it cannot be recalled, copied or forged.
7. Nothing in the valley flies. Seeded storms make the air over Calder unnavigable at any altitude an aircraft could use, so the core can be reached only on foot, across the ground the array governs.
8. Water in Calder runs where the array put it. Riverbeds, wells and the salt basin are all scheduled works, and any of them can be filled or emptied within hours by the core.
