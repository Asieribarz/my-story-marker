name: The Core Vault
---

**Appearance.** Cut into the root of the core mountain behind a single pressure door: a circular
chamber, cold, dry, lit blue by the governor lattice that fills its far wall — thousands of glass rods
in a steel cradle, each one lit from inside, shivering in patterns when the array speaks. In front of
the lattice, a low steel cradle with two hand grips worn smooth. To one side, the manual outfall valve:
a metre of cast wheel with its crank socket empty. Eleven years of dust on everything except the grips
and a folded jacket left on the floor.

**Atmosphere.** Silence after the corridor, so complete it rings. The chill of deep rock, the smell of
ozone and cold stone, a faint vibration through the soles. The room is not a lair; it is a workplace
somebody kept tidy, which is worse. The pressure door cycles slowly, audibly, and there is no second
exit.

**Narrative function.** The destination and the story's final choice. It converts the question from
"how do we shut it down" to "who stays", because the governor cannot be left unheld and the room
releases it only to a held hand. It is where Teo is finally a person rather than a voice, where Idris
takes the cradle, and where the valve is turned.
