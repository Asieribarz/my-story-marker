id: s3
name: The Drowned Orchard
---

**Appearance.** Two hundred hectares of pear and almond planted on the basin's eastern shelf, half of
it standing in permanent shallow water where a scheduled rain line falls every third day without fail,
the other half grey and dead beyond the line's edge. The boundary between them is as sharp as a ruled
mark. Sabel's holding sits at the centre: a stone well-house, a byre of condenser fabric, goats on the
raised paths, and a rack of salvaged tower parts sorted by size.

**Atmosphere.** Wet rot and blossom together, flies, dripping. Rain arrives on the hour with no cloud
worth the name, falls in a straight column, and stops. The place is proof that the array is neither
malicious nor careful — it is simply obeying a plan nobody updated, drowning one row and starving the
next. Under the trees it is cool and green and faintly obscene.

**Narrative function.** The human cost made local, and the only inhabited place on the route. It gives
the engineers a witness who has to live with their decision, supplies the manual valve crank that the
endgame requires, and is where the array stops scheduling and starts bargaining. It is also where
Maren must return at the end.
