name: The Iron Rain Corridor
---

**Appearance.** A nine-kilometre service cut running between the two densest tower ranks to the core
mountain: a raised cable road of steel grating, a handrail gone to rust, and above it the charged wire
that seeds the province's heavy weather. The towers here stand close enough to hum against one
another. Everything metal is pitted and orange; the rain that falls in the corridor carries iron
filings out of the condensers and stains the grating the colour of old blood.

**Atmosphere.** Loud. Continuous static discharge, wire singing, hail that arrives without warning and
stops as abruptly. The air smells of ozone and wet rust and tastes of metal at the back of the teeth.
There is no shelter and no way off the road but down into the tower field, and the corridor's weather
changes on the hour because someone is deciding it.

**Narrative function.** The physical gauntlet of the journey — the passage where the array's power is
felt on skin rather than argued about, and where the two engineers must cooperate perfectly while
trusting each other least. It is the last stretch of open ground before the core, and it is where
Sabel turns back.
