name: Idris Calloway
role: Co-protagonist; control engineer, co-designer of the array and the man who signed the recall order
desire: To close the array down quietly and leave the valley without the record ever being opened
fear: Being heard in Teo's own voice saying what he did, and found out by the one colleague he kept
arc: Begins as the charming, capable half of the partnership — the one who handles people, permits and paperwork, and who has spent eleven years managing Maren's questions rather than answering them. Under the array's patient pressure he first bargains, surrendering the master key to buy a safe corridor, then is stripped of every remaining evasion when the vault speaks. Ends by taking the governor with his own hands and holding it so that someone else can walk out — the first thing he has done in eleven years that cannot be renegotiated.
---

**Appearance.** Fifty, thick through the chest, still handsome in the way that has always worked for
him and has begun not to. Silver hair combed back, a close beard kept trimmed even on the fourth day
of a crossing. Pale, indoor skin burning badly across the nose in the basin light. Soft hands for an
engineer; a heavy signet ring he turns on his finger when he is about to say something untrue.

**Costume.** A good coat, too good — wool with a storm shell over it, bought for a city winter. A
leather document case slung crosswise that he never lets Maren carry, holding commissioning papers,
the recall order and the master key on a brass fob. City boots that ruin his feet by page four.

**Voice.** Warm, fluent, full of reassurance and the passive voice. He builds sentences that put no one
in them: "The decision was taken", "there was a window and it closed". He jokes when frightened and
calls Maren by her first name when he needs something. When the evasions finally run out his register
drops to something plain and unpractised, and it is the first time he sounds his age.
