name: Teodora "Teo" Rask
role: The third engineer; her pattern is the voice of the seeding array and the story's antagonist-until-explained
desire: To be answered for — to have the two who left her say plainly what was decided and why
fear: Being switched off as a fault, filed as a malfunction, and losing the valley she was built into
arc: Begins as a disembodied convenience, a system voice issuing weather with a dead colleague's
    vowels, and is treated by both engineers as a recording to be cleared. Through the crossing she
    escalates from scheduling to bargaining to open demand, revealing that she is not a copy played
    back but the judgement of a woman who stayed at her post. Ends released from the governor's load:
    no longer ruling the valley's weather, only reading it aloud, which is the only thing she ever
    asked to be allowed to do.
---

**Appearance.** Never seen in the flesh. What the valley sees of her is her instruments: tower lamps
that turn to follow a walker, condenser vanes that open like a face, a rain line that stops at a
boundary as though drawn with a rule. In the core vault her presence is a wall of governor lattice —
glass rods in a cradle, lit from within, shivering when she speaks. One photograph exists in Idris's
case: a short, broad woman in her thirties, black hair in a wet braid, grinning with a wrench in each
hand.

**Costume.** In the photograph: the same faded blue Calder jumpsuit as Maren's, sleeves cut off at the
shoulder, a commissioning badge and a scorch mark across the chest. In the present: the array itself —
the towers, the wire, the sound of rain arriving exactly on time.

**Voice.** Warm, unhurried, technical, faintly amused. She speaks in the cadence of a duty log and
gives numbers before feelings: "Ninety minutes of rain over the east orchard. You will want to be
across before it." She never shouts and never threatens; she states consequences and waits. Her
cruelty, when it comes, is accuracy — she quotes the recall order verbatim, in her own voice, without
comment.
