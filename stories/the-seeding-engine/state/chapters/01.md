Maren Vosk and Idris Calloway filed a descent from Kellin Ridge Station: two on foot, four days to the core vault, water for five. The relay answered in Teodora Rask's voice, corrected their route, named the seventh of March and the burn on Maren's left forearm as its own fault, and asked Idris whether he was bringing the papers. He did not say what was in the document case he has not let go of since the truck. On the pass Maren laid out the plan — twelve turns of scree, eighteen kilometres of basin, key in the door, the governor held while the load goes onto the manual crank, the lattice brought down through its own sequence. From the seventh turn the voice gave them forty minutes: the sluices had opened at ten past and the pass would flood to the twelfth turn. Idris answered in the passive voice; Teo recorded that nobody answered. They ran the last five turns onto the salt and watched the spillway take the cable gallery and put the station down the scree. The only relay out of Calder is gone, and a tower lamp has found them on the white.
