Two days of basin on the array's terms — fog to nine metres, glare at noon, eleven minutes of hail called in advance off a darkened tower head — brought them to the drowned orchard with three litres left. The well-keeper Sabel Onwe traded the byre and her well for a plain answer, and said the well-house speaker had told her three days earlier that two engineers were coming, Maren's name first. Under the scheduled rain the array sealed the east gate and the north cut, put the salt under four centimetres, and asked for the key. Idris laid the master key in the throat of a salvaged collector vane: by the commissioning rule the authority went with it permanently and beyond recall. Teo then read the recall order of the seventh of March in her own voice — the holding engineer withdrawn, no provision for return, signed I. Calloway, countersigned nobody — and opened a corridor from the byre gate at dawn, saying only one of them needed to arrive. Maren told Sabel the whole of it and was given the hundred-and-ten-kilo manual crank. Sabel will walk to the corridor mouth and no further.
