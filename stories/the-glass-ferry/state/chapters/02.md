An hour out, crossed water broke over the Strait's rocks. Kael ordered Orin to sheet in half a hand and steered for the deep channel between rocks and shoal. The ferry heeled; green water swept the deck and Orin fell against the cabin. Vex caught him by the collar, kept the documents clear, and told him to take a rope round his waist; the two worked the sheet together until the boat came through into slow swells.

Orin, lying on the deck, heard water rising through the boards. Kael handed the tiller to Vex, felt the hull, and found a long breach along the turn of the bilge. He could not stop it. Returning was two hours against the current and would sink her halfway; he ran on, giving her the hour she had left, and set Orin bailing to buy time, not to save her.

The ferry grounded on the far shore's sand. Vex said Paine had paid him; Kael confirmed it, and that he had brought them anyway. Vex went over the rail, crossed the shallows and took the path inland, unseen. Kael and Orin stayed as the Glass Ferry settled by the stern and went under.
