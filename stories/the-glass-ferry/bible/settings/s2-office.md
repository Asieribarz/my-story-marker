name: Merchant Paine's Office
---

**Appearance.** Occupies the upper floor of a stone building overlooking the harbor. Paneled in dark wood and appointed with the confidence of wealth. A large desk dominates the room, positioned so that light from the windows falls on anyone sitting across from it while leaving Paine in shadow. Maps and ledgers line the walls. The view through the windows shows ships in the harbor, commerce, and movement.

**Atmosphere.** Controlled, deliberate, and cold. There is no comfort here—chairs are hard, the temperature is cool, and every detail has been chosen to reinforce Paine's authority. The room itself seems to absorb sound, muffling voices so that confidences feel safer within its walls. It is a place designed to make a visitor feel small.

**Function.** The seat of power where the bargain is struck and where deals are made, money changes hands, and people's fates are decided. It is where Kael learns what he has agreed to do.
