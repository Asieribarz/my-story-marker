name: The Glass Ferry
---

**Appearance.** A small boat, perhaps thirty feet long, with a high prow carved with a pattern of waves that has been worn smooth by weather and time. The hull is oak, dark with age and treatment. The deck is narrow, with a small cabin aft that can barely accommodate two people standing upright. The name is painted in white on the bow. The boat is spotlessly maintained. There is a coil of rope, a sail carefully furled, a tiller for steering. Everything has its place and its purpose. The cabin smells of oil and wood and salt.

**Atmosphere.** Intimate space. There is nowhere to hide from another person, nowhere to avoid eye contact, nowhere to maintain a comfortable distance. Everyone aboard is intensely aware of everyone else. The boat moves through the water with a kind of grace despite its age, and the constant sounds of timber creaking, canvas snapping, and water moving become the primary focus of attention for those aboard.

**Function.** The enclosed space where intimacy forces confrontation and where bonds of loyalty are tested. The ferry enforces honesty because deception becomes physical when you cannot escape each other. It is Kael's home and his instrument, the only place where he is fully himself.
