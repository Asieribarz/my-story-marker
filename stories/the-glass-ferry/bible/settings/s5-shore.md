name: The Far Shore
---

**Appearance.** Less developed than the near shore. There are no docks, no merchants, no authority. The beach is rocky and steep, the land behind it rising into forested hills. A narrow path climbs from the beach into the mist. The vegetation here grows dense and wet, fed by the mist that never lifts from the Strait.

**Atmosphere.** One of freedom and anonymity. No one watches this shore. No customs, no records, no surveillance. It is the kind of place where a person can disappear and become someone new. But it is also wilderness—less comfort, less safety, less certainty.

**Function.** The destination that represents freedom for Vex and consequence for Kael. Reaching it means fulfilling Vex's escape and destroying Kael's livelihood. It is the physical manifestation of the choice between safety and integrity. Once the boat's hull touches the sand here, both Kael and Vex will have crossed a threshold that cannot be uncrossed.
