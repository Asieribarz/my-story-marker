name: The Strait
---

**Appearance.** A passage of water between two shores, perhaps a mile across at its narrowest point. The current runs swift and unpredictable, created by the interaction of two different bodies of water. Hidden rocks jut from the depths, invisible beneath the surface except when the water breaks across them in white foam. The mist rises from the water itself, thick and obscuring, never fully lifting. In the mist, the far shore is invisible. The passage takes between two and four hours depending on how the water moves.

**Atmosphere.** One of danger and isolation. The mist muffles sound. The boat becomes a small, fragile shell of wood in a much larger and more powerful world. There is no help coming from either shore. Sometimes the water is almost calm; sometimes it churns and throws the boat about. There is no way to predict what the crossing will bring.

**Function.** The external force that tests Kael and Vex, the barrier between the world of deception and the world of truth. The water is completely honest: it will do what it will do, and no amount of money or authority changes that. The Strait is where Kael is most alive and most himself, where every choice, every action, every moment counts.
