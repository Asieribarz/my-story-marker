**Appearance.** Sixty-three years old, weathered by three decades of wind and water. His hands are calloused and strong; his face is lined like a river map. He dresses in a gray wool coat, stained at the cuffs with river water that never quite washes out, and rope-soled boots that grip the deck. His hair has gone silver but his eyes are still sharp—the eyes of a man who reads the water the way others read books.

**Voice.** Speaks quietly, without flourish. His voice carries the rasp of someone who spends most of his days alone. When he makes a promise, he keeps it; when he says a crossing is safe, it is safe. This reputation is everything to him.
