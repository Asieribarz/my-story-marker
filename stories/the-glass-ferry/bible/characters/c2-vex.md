**Appearance.** Thirty-one years old, of indeterminate origin—they move through the world leaving little trace. Lean and careful in all movements, as though accustomed to taking up as little space as possible. Dress in dark, worn clothes that could belong to anyone; nothing about them is memorable except their eyes, which are watchful and intelligent. They carry nothing but the clothes on their body and a small bundle of documents.

**Voice.** Speaks only when necessary, and when they do, their words are chosen with precision. There is no waste in them—no chatter, no embellishment. They have learned to hide fear beneath stillness and to present a face of calm indifference even when their pulse is racing.
