**Appearance.** Twenty-two years old, the grandson of a friend of Kael's who died years ago. Broad-backed, strong, and eager, though not yet fully skilled at reading the water. His movements are efficient and practiced, but sometimes still clumsy with youth. He dresses in simple work clothes suitable for boat maintenance.

**Voice.** Speaks with the enthusiasm of youth, often asking questions before finishing the one before it. He sees Kael as a model of everything good and trustworthy. His voice carries innocence and hope, not yet tempered by disappointment.
