**Appearance.** Fifty-six years old, wealthy and accustomed to deference. Broad-shouldered, well-fed, and meticulously groomed. Dresses in fine wool and silk, decorated with rings that signal his wealth and importance. His hair is dark and slicked back, showing a forehead that has never known labor.

**Voice.** Speaks with the confidence of someone who has never been contradicted. His voice is rich and carries the weight of authority. He smiles when he threatens, and he smiles when he rewards. Both smiles are identical and contain no warmth.
