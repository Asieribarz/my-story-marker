Mara Vell came ashore at Ossary on the third day of the storm season and found the Cold Lamp dark three nights and not one boat on the quay willing to cross to it. The keepers' roll had her brother struck out on the eleventh, with a name she did not know written over his in newer ink, and Halloran Keeve would not say that name aloud in the harbour office or anywhere on the quay. Ester Quill, warden of Gannet Isle, ruled the brother drowned under article nine of the wreck law and offered Mara a paid passage home on Friday's tide and money at the other end for asking nothing further. Mara did not take it. She found instead the note of hand the warden holds over Halloran's boat - forty-two, bought off a chandler in the spring - and paid him thirty-one to cross the Weathering at the four o'clock slack, into the fourth day of the storm.
