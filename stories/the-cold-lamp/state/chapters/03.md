Dov Ashe brought the warden's lugger onto the Calder landing at first light to inventory the store under article four, and Mara lay hidden on the iron stair and heard him count it twice: thirty casks in the book and nineteen on the floor, eleven short. She came off his lugger at Gannet under a tarpaulin. In the lamp-wright's shed Nell Carrow told her what nobody at Ossary would: her brother is alive, and he stripped the reservoir and carried the oil off the Rock himself, and in the nine days since, two vessels have come onto the Drowned Stair in the dark. The eleven marked casks stood in the salvage yard behind the wall, entered in the yard ledger as wreck goods under article four on the fifth - three days before there was any wreck, and three days before the Cold Lamp went out. Ester Quill found her at the barrel head, confirmed the brother alive, and offered a berth on Friday's packet and a clean entry on the keepers' roll in exchange for her silence. Mara tore the page out along the stitching and ran for the gate.
