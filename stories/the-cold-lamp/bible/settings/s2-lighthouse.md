name: The Cold Lamp, Calder Rock
---

**Appearance.** A white tower eighty feet up from the landing, standing on bare rock with
no soil on it. A hundred and nine steps of iron stair. At the top, a lens the size of a
water butt in a brass frame, a reservoir under it, and a store of oil casks below that.

**Atmosphere.** Loud. Nothing on the Rock is still: the sea works under the landing and the
wind works on the glass, and in the dark the tower feels as though it is moving.

**Function.** The thing everyone in the story wants for a different reason - lit, dark, or
stripped - and the place the story ends.
