id: s4
name: The Drowned Stair
---

**Appearance.** A reef running out from Calder Rock in steps, cut square enough that people
insist it was built. At low water the first four steps stand clear. Below them the ground
falls away into forty feet of green water with the old quarry channel at the bottom of it.

**Atmosphere.** The only quiet place in the Banks, because the reef takes the sea's legs out
from under it. The quiet is the worst thing about it.

**Function.** The deep water. Where the fear that has been named since the first page has to
be gone down into instead of talked about.
