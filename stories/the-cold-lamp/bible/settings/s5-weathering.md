id: s5
name: The Weathering
---

**Appearance.** Two miles of open channel between Ossary and the outer Banks, with no
shelter anywhere in it and the deepest ground in the archipelago under the middle mile.

**Atmosphere.** A crossing everyone measures in minutes and nobody talks through. At slack
water it is flat and wrong-feeling; an hour later it stands up in short steep water coming
from two directions at once.

**Function.** The threshold. Crossed outward under commitment and back under proof, and shut
for a day at a time by the storm.
