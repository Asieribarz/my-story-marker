# World rules

r1. Nothing in the Banks is supernatural: every apparent marvel has a tidal, mechanical or human cause.
r2. The Cold Lamp burns whale-oil drawn from the keeper's store; with an empty reservoir it cannot be lit by any means.
r3. Each channel between the islands is passable only at slack water, twice a day and for less than an hour each time.
r4. Wreck law: whatever the sea puts ashore belongs to the warden of that island until a living owner claims it within nine days.
r5. The storm season runs nine days and worsens each day; it never breaks early.
r6. The Banks signal by bell alone - one stroke for slack water, two for a wreck, three for a death - and a bell carries further than any voice.
