**Appearance.** Twenty-two and salt-burned across the cheekbones. A salvage hook at the
belt, kept sharper than the work needs. A dead man's coat that does not fit him across the
shoulders.

**Voice.** Counts things aloud, and counts them twice. Says that is the order when he is
pressed. Will not finish a sentence that is about himself.
