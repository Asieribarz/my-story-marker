# Premise Expansion: Loro mudo

## Central Conflict

Sofía García has discovered an extraordinary gift: she alone can hear her parrot speak—a connection that reveals hidden truths and dark secrets about those around her. This bond isolates her from everyone who might explain what is happening, and makes her a target for anyone whose secrets the parrot might expose. When Sofía mysteriously disappears, El Loro becomes the sole witness to her fate, left with the burden of communicating a truth to a world that has always dismissed it as merely a bird.

## Theme

Truth cannot remain hidden; even the voiceless can bear witness. Connection transcends species and silence. Justice requires us to listen to evidence we do not expect to hear.

## Tone (as interpreted for this story)

Classic adventure infused with mystery and tension. The wonder of the parrot's gift is balanced against the danger it creates, giving the story an edge of magical realism. There is urgency, fear, and the cold logic of investigation meeting the warm certainty of an impossible bond. The resolution affirms that truth, once spoken, cannot be unheard.

## Tentative Ending

The parrot's cryptic communications—its patterns, sounds, and behaviors—guide Detective Ramírez and Dr. Chen to uncover what happened to Sofía. The antagonist is exposed through the parrot's testimony, recorded and analyzed by science. Sofía is found and reunited with El Loro. The story closes with the world finally believing what Sofía always knew: that the parrot speaks, that its gift is real, and that connection is the most powerful witness of all.
