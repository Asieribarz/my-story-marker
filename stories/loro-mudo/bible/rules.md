# World Rules

1. **Bound Speech**: The parrot can speak only to Sofía—its intelligence and articulate speech are gifts bound specifically to her, through a connection neither can explain or fully understand.

2. **Silent to Others**: No one else can hear the parrot's words. To all other people, El Loro produces only standard bird calls, squawks, and sounds. Its true nature is unknowable without proof.

3. **Limited Knowledge**: The parrot's knowledge extends only to what it has witnessed and what Sofía knew or wondered about. It cannot reveal information beyond these bounds, and cannot be forced to speak.

4. **Distress Signals**: The parrot becomes increasingly agitated, vocal, and visibly distressed when Sofía is in danger. Its behavior changes noticeably and dramatically, providing a physical warning that something is wrong.

5. **Safe Space Communication**: El Loro will communicate fully only in environments where it feels safe—primarily Sofía's apartment, where it has lived and been understood since it was young.

6. **Recorded Evidence**: Once the parrot's words are captured on audio or translated through Sofía's voice or another's interpretation, they become potential evidence. The world must learn to read a new language of truth.

7. **Fragile Gift**: The parrot's gift is not indestructible. Fear, prolonged separation from Sofía, repeated disbelief, and harsh skepticism can cause it to retreat into profound silence, withdrawing from communication entirely.

8. **Truth Requires Listening**: The parrot's insights can solve mysteries and expose injustice, but only if people are willing to listen carefully, set aside their doubts, and choose to believe what seems impossible.
