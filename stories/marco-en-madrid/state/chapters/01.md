Marco Domínguez arrives in Madrid from Lima with a signed contract from an architecture firm and high hopes for his future. He meets Elena, a woman who agrees to shelter him, and experiences the initial euphoria of a carefully planned beginning. However, on his second day, Marco discovers the firm has collapsed—the job that justified his journey no longer exists. Stranded with a tourist visa and a family in Peru expecting his success, he faces an impossible choice: reveal the failure or invent a new story.

Marco chooses the lie. He tells his mother everything is proceeding perfectly while secretly taking undeclared construction work. He meets Javier, a Colombian laborer, who becomes his friend and mentor in the world of illegal employment. Marco discovers he possesses architectural knowledge even as construction work, and through Javier's connections, he moves from Elena's apartment into a basement room shared with other undeclared workers.

The chapter ends with Marco committed to a divided existence: the architect his family believes him to be in Lima, and the construction laborer he has become in Madrid. His hands are raw. His visa is expiring. The lie grows with each contact.
