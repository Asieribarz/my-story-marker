name: Madrid — the city streets
---

**Appearance.** Madrid in early autumn. Neighborhoods of older buildings with interior patios, narrow streets where the sun reaches only between 2 and 4 PM. Metro stations crowded with people moving to jobs they are not excited about. Cafés where people sit for hours over one coffee. Construction sites where buildings are being demolished or renovated—a visual metaphor for transformation.

**Atmosphere.** Simultaneously beautiful and hostile. Madrid is a city that does not care about Marco's story. It moves with its own logic: employment, commerce, reproduction, indifference. There is freedom in this indifference, but also loneliness. The city is full of strangers, and Marco will remain a stranger until he stops needing validation that he is not.

**Function.** The vast, indifferent stage where Marco is simultaneously invisible and exposed
