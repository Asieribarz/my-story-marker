name: Immigration and labor office — the system made visible
---

**Appearance.** A functional office in a modern building. Fluorescent lights, plastic chairs bolted to the floor, a window with a number display showing whose turn it is. Forms in Spanish, English, French, Arabic. A faint smell of cleaning products and resignation. The walls are beige and bare except for official notices in small print.

**Atmosphere.** Sterile and dispiriting. This is where individual stories cease to matter and are replaced by procedure. Every interaction here strips Marco of agency—he is a file number, a submitted form, a decision pending. The office does not reject him personally; it does not acknowledge his personhood at all. This is both the source of Marco's rage and eventually, the source of his liberation: he was never the system's concern, which means the system cannot define him.

**Function.** The embodied resistance of institutional indifference; where Marco's hopes confront official reality
