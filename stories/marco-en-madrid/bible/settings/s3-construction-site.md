id: s3
name: A Madrid construction site — temporary labor
---

**Appearance.** Scaffolding and exposed brick. The skeleton of a building being renovated. Dust everywhere. A portable toilet and a small metal shed for tools and breaks. The site changes weekly—the building grows or empties, the team rotates, nothing is permanent. In the distance, the real Madrid: finished buildings, people in offices, the world Marco was supposed to inhabit.

**Atmosphere.** Physically demanding and psychologically deadening. Marco watches architects on site planning the renovation he is helping to execute with his hands, not his mind. The work is honest but it is not his. There is camaraderie among the workers—they are all displaced, all temporary—but the camaraderie itself is a reminder that Marco is no longer an architect; he is a laborer. This is not failure in the traditional sense; it is identity death in slow motion.

**Function.** Where Marco's hands do work his mind rejects; where he encounters other displaced people; where his architect's eye watches what he cannot create
