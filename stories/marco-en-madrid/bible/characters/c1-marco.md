**Appearance.** Mid-thirties, with the careful posture of someone trained to be competent. Thick dark hair always neat. He arrived in Madrid in pressed shirts and Italian leather shoes—the uniform of professional success. As weeks pass, his clothes wear, the shoes crack, and he dresses in borrowed, ill-fitting layers. His hands, always architectural in their precision, become rough from manual work.

**Voice.** Precise Spanish with a Peruvian lilt that sharpens when he is defensive. Speaks in complete sentences, dislikes slang. When tired or discouraged, his voice flattens and loses its music. When speaking to his family by phone (rare), he switches to a false brightness that exhausts him.
