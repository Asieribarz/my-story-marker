**Appearance.** Sixty-two, present only through phone calls and brief messages. Marco remembers her in the clothes she wore to church—careful, modest, always appropriate. Her voice carries the weight of the Lima she knows, not the Madrid Marco inhabits.

**Voice.** Warm but interrogative. She speaks in the past tense, anchoring every conversation in family history. She laughs easily but her laughter has an edge—she is a woman who has survived by knowing what people are not saying. She phrases questions as statements: "You sound tired" means "Are you okay?"
