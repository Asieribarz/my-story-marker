**Appearance.** Late fifties, with the carefully neutral appearance of a government official. He wears the same style of shirt in three colors—navy, gray, white. He is neither unkind nor warm. His office is impersonal, decorated only with necessary documents and a small photo of a family.

**Voice.** Formal and measured. He speaks in the passive voice whenever possible—"applications are processed," "decisions are made." He rarely uses names, preferring titles or numbers. When he does make eye contact, it feels accidental.
