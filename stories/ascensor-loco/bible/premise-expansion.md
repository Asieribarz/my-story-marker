# Premise Expansion: Ascensor loco

## Central Conflict

Ana García, a visitor to a centuries-old building in an unnamed city, takes an elevator that connects floors that do not appear on any floor plan. Each impossible journey reveals a secret about the building's hidden purpose, about Miguel Sánchez the operator's true role, about Dr. Castellanos's control and surveillance, about Tomás who has been imprisoned in the building for years, and ultimately about Rosa Mendez, the architect who designed the elevator to be exactly this way. Ana must navigate the tension between pursuing truth and honoring the secrets that people will risk everything to protect. Her final choice—whether to expose the building's impossible nature to the world or become complicit in its concealment—will decide not only her fate but the fate of everyone trapped within the building's walls.

## Theme

Hidden truths become impossible to ignore once glimpsed. Knowledge brings responsibility, and the line between safety and imprisonment is thinner than we believe. The story explores how impossible things become normalized when no one dares to question them, and what one person must sacrifice to change that equilibrium.

## Tone

Surreal and mysterious, grounded in real human consequences. The impossible spaces have a dreamlike quality—they bend perception and confound time—but the people within them face genuine danger and desperation. The mood begins in curiosity and wonder, deepens into disorientation and danger, and culminates in revelation and moral urgency.

## Tentative Ending

Ana reaches the rooftop in the heart of the night, where she encounters Rosa Mendez. Rosa reveals that the elevator was designed deliberately, not as malfunction but as intent. The building is an experiment, a sanctuary, a cage, or something beyond those categories—the design itself was an act of rebellion or control, depending on the perspective. Ana faces her choice: expose the building's secret to authorities and destroy its balance, potentially freeing Tomás and others but exposing the building to destruction or corruption; or protect the secret and become a guardian like Miguel, forever bound to the building's impossible logic. In the final image, Ana descends in the elevator one last time. She is transformed, aware of truths the world outside cannot comprehend, and complicit in a secret that will follow her for the rest of her life.
