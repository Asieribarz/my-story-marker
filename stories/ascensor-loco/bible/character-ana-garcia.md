# Character: Ana García

## Header

**ID**: ana-garcia  
**Name**: Ana García  
**Role**: The Protagonist and Visitor  

**Arc**: Ana begins as an intelligent, curious outsider who enters the building skeptical of anything she cannot explain. She believes the world operates on rational principles and that mysteries have logical solutions. By the midpoint, she is forced to abandon that skepticism and accept that the impossible is real and intentional. By the end, she has transcended the desire for logical explanation and must instead choose between complicity and exposure—a moral choice, not an intellectual one.

**Initial State**: Rational, skeptical, an outsider seeking answers  
**Final State**: Transformed, bearing knowledge that isolates her, complicit in a secret she cannot share

**Desire**: To understand the building's secrets and prove they are real  
**Fear**: That she is losing her mind; that there is no logic to the world at all

## Body

**Appearance**: Ana is in her late twenties, with dark hair usually pulled back, sharp eyes that miss nothing. She dresses simply but with intention—practical shoes, a notebook always in her bag. She has the physical presence of someone used to moving through the world alone, taking up minimal space, watching before she acts.

**Costume**: A gray or olive jacket that she wears over casual layers. The notebook is visible, often in her hands. She carries a phone but doesn't rely on it—it has no signal in the building.

**Voice**: Ana speaks carefully, often asking questions rather than making statements. Her tone is steady and analytical, but there are moments when her composure cracks and her fear shows. As the story progresses, her voice becomes less certain, more urgent. By the end, she speaks with the weight of knowledge she cannot share.

**Narrative Function**: Ana is the reader's surrogate. Her journey from skepticism to acceptance mirrors the audience's own disbelief transforming into wonder and then dread. Every beat of the story is measured against her reaction and her understanding. She is the conscience of the narrative, forced to make impossible choices.

**K1–K7 Thread**: Ana's arc runs through every page. Page 1: curiosity and skepticism. Page 2: first doubt about reality. Page 3: commitment and no turning back. Page 4: deepening discovery and loss of certainty. Page 5: confrontation with power and danger. Page 6: final revelation and moral choice.
