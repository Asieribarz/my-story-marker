# Character: Dr. Castellanos

## Header

**ID**: dr-castellanos  
**Name**: Dr. Castellanos  
**Role**: The Administrator and Defender of Official Truth  

**Arc**: Dr. Castellanos is the building's official authority—she maintains the records, controls access, and enforces the version of reality that keeps the building functioning. She appears at first as merely bureaucratic and stern, but is revealed to be actively suppressing the truth and possibly actively dangerous in her devotion to control. She represents institutional power defending itself against exposure. By the end, she is either defeated by Ana's knowledge or she silences the threat.

**Initial State**: Stern, bureaucratic, dismissive of anomalies  
**Final State**: Confronted with irrefutable truth; her authority challenged or her power revealed

**Desire**: To maintain control of the building's narrative and reputation  
**Fear**: That exposure will destroy everything she has built or that she will be held accountable for crimes of concealment

## Body

**Appearance**: Dr. Castellanos is in her sixties, with steel-gray hair cut precisely, severe glasses, and the posture of someone accustomed to authority. She is thin and angular, moving with economical gestures. Her clothing is formal, expensive, slightly outdated—a woman of the previous generation clinging to the aesthetics of power.

**Costume**: A tailored suit in dark colors, always with a small pin or brooch that suggests military or institutional service. A key on a chain at her waist—the key to the archive, the key to records. She never appears without this marker of authority.

**Voice**: Dr. Castellanos speaks with absolute certainty, each sentence a statement of fact, not opinion. She uses official terminology and passive constructions that distance her from responsibility. When challenged, her voice becomes cold and precise, like a blade. There is no warmth in her speech, only efficiency and authority.

**Narrative Function**: Dr. Castellanos is the story's institutional antagonist. She does not act from malice but from the logic of institutions—the need to protect the system above all else. She forces Ana into direct conflict with organized power, making clear that knowledge alone is not enough; truth must be defended against those who benefit from lies.

**K1–K7 Thread**: Dr. Castellanos is absent from pages 1–2 but her presence is felt in the building's official records. Page 3: she observes Ana's inquiry. Page 4: she monitors Ana's movements. Page 5: she confronts Ana directly in the archive, creating the story's climactic conflict. She may appear in page 6 or remain absent, her judgment rendered by Ana's choice.
