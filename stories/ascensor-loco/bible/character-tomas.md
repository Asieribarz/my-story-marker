# Character: Tomás

## Header

**ID**: tomas  
**Name**: Tomás  
**Role**: The Long-Term Resident and Prisoner  

**Arc**: Tomás has lived in the building for so long that he no longer remembers life outside it. He is dependent on the building and the elevator for his survival, but also trapped by that same dependency. He represents the story's cautionary outcome—what happens to people who accept the impossible for long enough. Meeting Ana offers him possibility and danger in equal measure: she might free him, or she might destroy the only world he has left.

**Initial State**: Resigned to his situation, normalized to the impossible, dependent but stable  
**Final State**: Either freed by Ana's choice or confirmed in his imprisonment; changed by the knowledge that escape might have been possible

**Desire**: To escape the building, or to remain safe within it—he oscillates between the two  
**Fear**: Change; the unknown outside; being forgotten if he stays; being expelled if he tries to leave

## Body

**Appearance**: Tomás is difficult to place in age—he could be anywhere from fifty to seventy, worn thin by years of institutional living. His clothing is clean but worn through many washings, slightly too large on his frame. His hands are marked by work, his face by sun from the building's high windows. He moves carefully, as though accustomed to taking up as little space as possible.

**Costume**: Simple worker's clothing—a shirt and pants worn soft by time. A key on a cord around his neck, suggesting maintenance duties or access to certain areas. His appearance suggests someone in service, someone accustomed to invisibility.

**Voice**: Tomás speaks in a quiet voice, with long pauses between thoughts, as though he is remembering the habit of speech. His accent suggests a different era, perhaps a different place. When he speaks of the building, his voice becomes almost reverent—he has accepted it as reality and perhaps as home.

**Narrative Function**: Tomás is the story's human consequence. He shows Ana what the building costs, what it demands from those who choose to live within it. He represents the stakes of her choice—if she exposes the building, she frees Tomás but destroys his world. If she protects it, she condemns him to continued imprisonment. He is the moral weight on Ana's final decision.

**K1–K7 Thread**: Tomás appears primarily in pages 2 and 4, meeting Ana on the impossible floors and showing her how long-term inhabitants relate to the building's strangeness. His presence in the archive (page 4) or his absence from page 6 will shape Ana's final choice.
