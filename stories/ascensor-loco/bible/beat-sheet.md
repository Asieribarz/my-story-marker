## Page 4 | Chapter 2: "The Descent" | Act 2 (Development) | Anchor: No

**Objective**: Ana descends via the elevator to an impossible level and accesses the archive, discovering architectural plans and evidence that the elevator was not a malfunction but a deliberate design—and that the architect, Rosa Mendez, is still somehow present in the building's secrets.

**Closing Hook**: Ana finds Rosa's name on the plans, connecting the building's impossible geometry to a single person's intentional design.

**Characters**: ana-garcia, miguel-sanchez  
**Settings**: elevator, archive

## Page 5 | Chapter 2: "The Descent" | Act 3 (Resolution) | Anchor: No

**Closing Hook**: Ana reaches the elevator and ascends toward the rooftop, no longer fleeing from the building's secrets but toward them.

**Characters**: ana-garcia, dr-castellanos, miguel-sanchez

## Page 6 | Chapter 2: "The Descent" | Act 3 (Resolution) | Anchor: YES

**Objective**: Ana reaches the rooftop and meets Rosa Mendez, who reveals the building's true nature and purpose; Ana faces her final choice—expose the building's secret to authorities and destroy its hidden world, or protect the secret and become forever bound to the building's impossible logic—a choice that irrevocably transforms her.

**Closing Hook**: Ana makes her decision and descends in the elevator one final time, carrying knowledge that isolates her and commits her to a secret she can never fully share.

**Characters**: ana-garcia, rosa-mendez, miguel-sanchez  
**Settings**: rooftop, elevator  

**Reversal**: Ana moves beyond seeking truth to bearing the weight of truth. She chooses her role in the building's future—whether as exposer or guardian—and that choice defines the rest of her life. She exits the elevator transformed, no longer the skeptical visitor but a person who knows the impossible is real and must live with that knowledge.
