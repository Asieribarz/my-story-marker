# World Rules: Ascensor loco

## What Is Possible

1. **Elevator Access to Impossible Floors**: The elevator's shaft is geometrically fixed in the building, but the floors it accesses shift and do not correspond to the official floor plan. The elevator can reach spaces that are not marked, not documented, and not believed to exist by the building's management. This is not malfunction—it is the elevator's intended design.

2. **One Journey per Destination**: The elevator will never take a passenger to the same impossible floor twice. Each journey is unique and irreplaceable. Once a floor is visited, it is closed to that passenger forever, creating a scarcity of knowledge and forcing immediate decisions about exploration.

3. **Temporal Disorientation**: Time inside the elevator passes at a normal rate by objective measure, but passengers emerge with profound disorientation about the duration of their journey. Five minutes may feel like five hours, or vice versa. Passengers cannot trust their internal sense of time, creating a persistent sense of unreality.

4. **Building Reconfiguration**: Physical spaces inside the building subtly reorganize and reconfigure around the elevator's destinations. Walls shift, corridors reform, and the building's internal logic follows the elevator's journeys rather than resisting them, as if the structure is alive and reshaping to accommodate the elevator's purpose.

5. **Selective Passage**: The elevator will carry a passenger to a floor, but only if there is something there meant to be discovered. The elevator refuses to take passengers to destinations where they are not ready or not meant to know the truth. This is a form of intention built into the machine.

## What Is Forbidden

1. **No Questioning from Inhabitants**: No one who has lived or worked in the building for more than a few years will question the elevator's behavior. Asking "Is this normal?" to a longtime resident results only in silence or confusion. The building's inhabitants have normalized the impossible through long habituation.

2. **Documentation Cannot Match Reality**: Written records in the building—floor plans, registries, architectural drawings, access logs—systematically contradict the building's actual layout and the elevator's destinations. No amount of searching through records will resolve the gap between the official building and the actual building. The two truths are irreconcilable.

3. **The Elevator Cannot Be Stopped**: Once the elevator is summoned, it cannot be called back. The passenger must complete the journey. Attempting to stop the elevator between floors results only in continued motion—the doors will not open, and resistance is futile.

4. **No Escape Without Descent**: There is no way to leave the building except through the elevator. Other exits are locked, bricked over, or simply do not lead outside. The elevator is the only path to freedom, and it is also the only path deeper into the building's secrets.
