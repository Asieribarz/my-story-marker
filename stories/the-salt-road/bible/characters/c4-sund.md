**Appearance.** Fifty, and office clothes kept clean on a salt coast, which takes a servant
and is meant to be noticed. Ink along the outside edge of the right hand. A seal ring worn
on a cord rather than a finger, because his fingers have swollen and he will not have the
ring cut.

**Voice.** Quotes the article number before he gives the rule. Calls people by their trade
and not by their name. Never raises his voice, and closes what he says with "as the book has
it".
