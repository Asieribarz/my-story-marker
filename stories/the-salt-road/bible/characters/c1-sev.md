**Appearance.** Forty, and salt has had all of it. Hands cracked white across the knuckles
and mended with tallow. A grey canvas coat gone white along every seam. At her belt a
guide's tally-cord of knotted twine, eleven knots in it, none of them undone.

**Voice.** Counts aloud when she is frightened, and does not know she is doing it. Gives
distance in paces and never in hours. Will not use the word lost, about the Pan or about
anything else.
