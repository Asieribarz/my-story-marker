**Appearance.** Twelve years old, or near enough that nobody at Dray Landing argues about
it. A brine burn round the throat like a collar, pale against the rest of her. A tin
water-can she does not set down even to sleep, and Kethra's tally-case on a cord under her
shirt.

**Voice.** She does not speak and cannot. Two taps on the can for yes, one for no, and
nothing at all for a question she has decided not to answer. Writes numbers with one finger
in the salt and rubs them out herself as soon as they are read. Never gives the same sign
twice running.
