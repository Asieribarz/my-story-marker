**Appearance.** Sixty, big through the shoulders and slow with it. One eye gone milky from
a season of glare. A drover's quirt he carries as a pointer and has never once laid on a
beast.

**Voice.** Talks to the camels about the people and to the people about the camels. Puts a
price on everything aloud, including things that are not for sale. Answers what he does not
believe with "that is a Dray Landing answer".
