# Premise expansion

**Premise (run argument).** A disgraced salt-caravan guide recrosses a drying inland sea to
learn why a well-town has stopped lodging its water tally, taking a child who knows the
line of cairns by heart and has no voice to say it.

## Central conflict

Sev Marrow must cross the Pan and come back inside the last days of the season, over a
salt crust that bears weight only in the cold dark, along a line of cairns that cannot be
seen except at first light. Those two rules contradict each other, and the contradiction is
what killed eleven people under her eleven years ago. Against her stands Berec Sund, factor
of the Salt Office at Dray Landing, who has been drawing Kethra's water and selling it at
the landing under his own seal, and who needs the line to lapse before anybody lodges a
count that does not match his stamps. Against her also stands what she finds at the far
side: Kethra walked out two seasons ago and is standing on the Dray Landing dock under
another name, the map-keeper stayed behind to keep the count and drank the well until it
killed him, and the only copy of the count is in the head of the apprentice he swore in
before he died — a girl who stopped drinking in time to keep everything but her voice.

## The arithmetic

Water is carried, or it is drawn at the two cisterns of the line, Cairn Four and Cairn
Nine, and nowhere else. The legs are not equal: Four is close in, Nine is a night out, and
Kethra is a night beyond Nine.

| Leg | Cold windows | Water |
|---|---|---|
| Dray Landing to Cairn Nine, through Four | one night | carried from the landing, drawn full again at Four on the way through |
| Cairn Nine to Kethra | the next night | Nine is found drawn out and gives nothing, so this is the Four load: enough to go on, or back to Four, not both |
| Kethra to Cairn Four, past a dry Nine | a night, a day lying up on the crust, and most of the next night | Kethra's last sealed cistern, and not a swallow past Four |
| Cairn Four to Dray Landing | the soft third of the morning | Cairn Four's cistern, carried in on foot: after sunrise r2 leaves no other way to move |

Everything after page five turns on the last two rows, and the reader should be able to do
the sum alongside the characters. The last row is the price: the crust that will not carry
a loaded camel after sunrise is why the camels are cut loose and the party walks in.

## Theme

What a person is owed does not depend on their being able to ask for it. A count is kept
for people who are not in the room, and keeping it is the whole of the work.

## Tone, as interpreted for this story

Classic adventure: plain, concrete, forward-moving prose. Distance, water and cold are the
first antagonists and are never metaphorical. Danger here is arithmetic — paces against
water, dark against light, the count of casks. No irony, no knowingness, no comedy at the
expense of the stakes. The prose tightens as the light comes up. The clerkwork is carried in
action: a seal, a nail, a name written in a book, never in a lecture.

## Tentative ending

On the long stage back the count runs out in the dark with no cairn in it. At first light
there is nothing to steer by, and Sev takes the party off the line on purpose — the one
thing she swore never to do again — on the bearing and the pace-count she came in on when
she walked off the Pan alone, to the only mark out there, which is the wreck of her own
caravan. She paces the bearing back off it, cross-fixes the slab, and her eleven dead give
the party its water. Oduin cuts his two camels loose rather than watch them go through the
softening crust, and the three of them walk the short leg in on foot and come up the ramp
an hour short of noon, past the Kethra hands working the dock. Behind the lectern the
Kethra nail is empty, and the only paper on the board under it is an order striking Cairn
Four from the line for repair, dated the night they went out: the cairn stood when they
passed it and was taken down while they were across, and the word repair is the whole of
the cover. Sund refuses the lodgement on two articles, the void case and the voiceless
keeper. Tallow answers both with the only credential the story allows: she writes the count
into the book in her own hand, and nobody but Kethra's keeper could have it. The seals on
Sund's own nails cannot match it, and he is struck off under the article he was quoting.
Sev signs beneath the count and puts the eleven names into the book with her own and
Oduin's. She takes the line itself as its keeper, and goes out on to the Pan in the cold to
build Cairn Four up again.
