name: The Pan
---

**Appearance.** Eighty miles of white, flat to the edge of sight in every direction, broken
into plates the size of a table by ridges of thrown-up salt a hand high. Under the crust,
brine. Nothing grows on it and nothing crosses it that is not put there.

**Atmosphere.** Cold enough at night to crack the lips, and loud underfoot: the crust rings
and ticks as it cools and again as it warms, so a party always knows, by sound, how much of
the night it has left. In the dark there is nothing to look at and the party walks on a
count. At first light there is too much to look at and none of it is a landmark.

**Function.** The antagonist that cannot be argued with, and the arithmetic the whole story
is made of — paces against water, dark against light.
