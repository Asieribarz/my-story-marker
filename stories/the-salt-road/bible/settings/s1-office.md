name: The Salt Office, Dray Landing
---

**Appearance.** A long stone room at the head of the loading ramp, with the tally book open
on a lectern at the far end and the season's cistern seals hung in rows on the wall behind
it, each on its own nail. Salt comes in on every boot and is swept out twice a day, and the
floor is worn pale in a track from the door to the lectern.

**Atmosphere.** Quiet in a way that is kept rather than natural. Everybody in the room can
hear the pen. What is decided here is decided by an entry, not by an argument, and
everyone waiting knows it.

**Function.** Where the Pan is turned into numbers, and therefore where the story can be won
without anybody crossing anything. It is the start and the end.
