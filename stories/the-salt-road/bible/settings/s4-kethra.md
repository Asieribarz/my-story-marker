id: s4
name: Kethra
---

**Appearance.** A well-town of forty flat roofs on the only rise at the far side, built in a
ring round a wellhead with a windlass over it. The tally house stands against the wellhead
wall: one room, the town's ledgers in a chest, the season's cistern seals in a rack above
them, and under the floor the town's own sealed cistern, which is the last water in Kethra
and will carry a party as far as Cairn Four and not a swallow past it.

**Atmosphere.** Swept, shuttered and in order, which is what makes it terrible: nothing here
was abandoned in a hurry. The windlass rope is coiled, every door is barred on the outside
by the people who lived behind it, and the well when a stone is dropped in it answers wrong.

**Function.** The far end of the crossing and the turn of the story: what the party came to
collect is not here, and what is here has to be carried back.
