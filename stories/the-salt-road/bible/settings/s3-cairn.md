---
id: s3
name: The cistern cairns, Four and Nine
---

**Appearance.** A cone of salt blocks the height of a standing man, whitewashed on the
landing side so that it takes first light before anything else on the Pan does. At its foot
a slab, and under the slab a shaft cut into the hard salt to a cistern the size of a cart,
lined and sealed, the seal carrying the year and the Office stamp. There are two of these
on the line and no more. Cairn Four stands a third of a cold window out from Dray
Landing and Cairn Nine two thirds of a window beyond Four, so the landing and Nine are one
night apart with Four on the way through. Kethra is a full window beyond Nine.

**Atmosphere.** The only thing out here a person did not bring with them, and the relief of
reaching one is out of all proportion to what it is, which is a heap of blocks. Standing in
its shadow a party talks more than it has for hours.

**Function.** The water the arithmetic of the crossing is built on. The cairn is also the
only thing that finds the slab: without the cone standing over it, the lid of a cistern is
one white plate among a million, and a party can be standing on its water and not know.
