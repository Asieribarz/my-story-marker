At first light nothing took the light where Cairn Four's whitewash should have taken it first. Sev named what lay off the line - her own caravan, eleven people, eleven years - put the party on the bearing and pace-count she had carried since, and let the softening crust close the line behind them. The wreck came up at nineteen hundred paces with her own tally-cord still knotted to the rope, and she ran the bearing back off it on to Cairn Four's slab. They drew, weighted the slab back, and when the crust began to let the loaded beasts through Oduin cut the loads off and turned the camels loose light. The three walked in on their own legs, wet to the thigh, the whole of the morning, past the Kethra hands at the foot of the ramp, with an hour left. Behind the lectern the Kethra nail was empty and an order struck Cairn Four from the line for repair, dated the night they went out. Sund refused the lodgement on two articles. Tallow answered with the ledger and the pen, and the count named who had last drawn.
