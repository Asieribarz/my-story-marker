The second cast held, the crews lashed to their thwarts. Three turns of hair-rope took her at the waist and she did not break them. In the boat Ignacio saw the healed tear from wrist to elbow on her left forearm and knew the catch of eleven years was the creditor of eleven years. He ordered her into the lead tank and did not touch the rope again; Fausto took the half-hour glass off its nail and counted from the moment she left the water. Six days, the tally chalked on the beam: she sang at thirty turns, seldom at a hundred, and by two hundred and forty there was breath and no voice, the loss steady and not to be slowed. Pau changed the stale water eleven times a day and asked who the proving was for, now that she was spending herself to pay for it, and got no answer. On the seventh evening Fausto set course for the Guadalquivir and a hall behind the Lonja with eleven witnesses. Brites named rule seven and rule four against it and asked for an order. Ignacio gave none. At first light Pau raised the bar.
