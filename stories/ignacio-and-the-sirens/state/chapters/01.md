Ignacio Robles bought the last nine pounds of hair-rope in Cadiz and signed Don Fausto Delgado's bond: a living specimen, whole and breathing, before the financier's witnesses, on forfeit of the ship in sixty-one days. Fausto sailed with them. Three days out Brites Olaya made him tell the boy the story straight. At twenty he went over off the Restinga, could not swim, and was held up from behind a night and a day by something that breathed; as the Sanlucar boat came up he saw an arm go down past his leg with a healed tear from wrist to elbow. Four men in that boat gave four accounts, which is rule four, and is why he wants a body and not a memory. They raised the shoal on the nineteenth, eleven years to the day. Pau felt the song in the bone, not the ear; Ignacio put both boats down, and Brites cut off the plait of fifteen years for rope without saying what frightened her. The first cast failed inside the song: the boats came off the line, Brites went over the side, was hauled back after eleven seconds, and could not say what she had seen.
