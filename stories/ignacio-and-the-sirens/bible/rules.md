# World rules

r1. Mermaids sing only over ground shallow enough to see bottom; in deep water they are silent, and no one has found one there.
r2. The song is not heard by the ear but felt in the bone. Wax, cloth and stopped ears are no defence; distance and deep water are the only defences.
r3. Out of the open sea a mermaid loses her voice at a steady rate, whatever water she is kept in. The loss is counted in turns of the half-hour glass, it cannot be slowed, and once the voice is gone it does not come back.
r4. No two people have ever seen a mermaid and afterwards agreed on what they saw. This is not a saying. It has been tested.
r5. Iron and hemp will not hold them. Rope woven of human hair will, for as long as it is unbroken.
r6. A mermaid who has held a life out of the water must surface again at that same place a year to the day.
r7. None of them will cross the bar where a river's fresh water meets the sea, and none has ever been taken past it alive.
