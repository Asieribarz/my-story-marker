**Appearance.** Smaller than the stories have her. Skin the colour of wet shale, going grey
and papery within the hour out of the sea. Eyes with no white to them. A healed tear across
the left forearm, eleven years old, which is how Ignacio knows her.

**Costume.** Nothing, except the hair-rope once it is on her, three turns at the waist.

**Voice.** Does not speak in any language of the coast. Sings, and the singing is felt in
the bone (r2); answers questions by repeating back the pitch of the voice that asked them.
As her voice goes she answers lower, then only in breath, then not at all.
