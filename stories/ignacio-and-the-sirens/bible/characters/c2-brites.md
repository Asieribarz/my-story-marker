**Appearance.** First mate, fifteen years at sea before she met Ignacio. Grey coming in at
the temples, hands ruined by rope, a seaman's plait she has never cut because rule five
takes hair and she gives her own when the ship is short.

**Costume.** Carries the knife that cuts the hair-rope. It has no other use aboard and she
sharpens it anyway.

**Voice.** Short. Says the cost of a thing before she says anything else about it. Calls
Ignacio "captain" when she agrees with him and "Robles" when she does not.
