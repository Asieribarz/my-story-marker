**Appearance.** Thirty-one, and weathered past it. Black beard kept short because a long
one freezes. A white band of unburnt skin across the back of the neck where the cord of
his hat has sat for eleven years. Coat too good for the ship, bought in the year he still
had a patron's good opinion.

**Costume.** A half-hour glass on a lanyard, worn under the shirt against the skin. He
turns it without noticing he has done it.

**Voice.** Speaks in working orders even when nothing is being worked. Will not use the
word mermaid; says "she", or "the catch". Answers a question about himself by giving a
bearing.
