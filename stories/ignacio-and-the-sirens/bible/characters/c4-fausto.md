**Appearance.** Sixty, dry, seasick from the first hour and never once complains of it. A
good hat he will not take off on deck. Keeps his accounts in a ledger with a brass lock and
does his own sums.

**Costume.** The bond with Ignacio's name on it, folded in the ledger, produced at each
disagreement like a chart.

**Voice.** Courteous, complete sentences, never raises his voice. Speaks of Yara as "it".
Reasonable in a way that leaves nothing to answer.
