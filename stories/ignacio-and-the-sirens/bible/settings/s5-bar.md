name: The Guadalquivir bar
---

**Appearance.** A brown line across green water, half a mile wide, where the river's outfall
meets the sea. Visible from the masthead as a seam.

**Atmosphere.** The one place in the story where the world rules and the men's intentions
are in the same room.

**Function.** The limit (r7). Everything is decided on the seaward side of it.
