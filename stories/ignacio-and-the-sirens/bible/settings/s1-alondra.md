id: s1
name: La Alondra
---

**Appearance.** A sixty-ton caravel, patched at the waterline, rigged for shoal work with
two boats on the deck where cargo should be. Her hold has been fitted with a lead-lined
tank that cost more than the rest of her.

**Atmosphere.** A ship with no cargo is a ship with too much room, and everyone aboard
feels watched by the space.

**Function.** The instrument of the hunt, and afterwards the prison.
