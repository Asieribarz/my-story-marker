name: Cadiz harbour
---

**Appearance.** Chandleries, bond offices and a rope-walk where a woman buys cut hair by
weight and will not say what for. White light off the water and off the walls.

**Atmosphere.** A town that has heard every sailor's story and priced each one.

**Function.** Where the voyage is bought, and where it comes back to be disbelieved.
