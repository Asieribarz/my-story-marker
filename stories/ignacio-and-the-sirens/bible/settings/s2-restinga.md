id: s2
name: La Restinga, the Sirens' Shoal
---

**Appearance.** Nine miles of pale ground two fathoms under, with the bottom visible the
whole way across on a clear day - sand, weed lanes, and the dark ribs of older wrecks.
Deep water on three sides of it.

**Atmosphere.** Bright, shallow, entirely open. Nothing hides here, which is why it is the
only place they are ever heard (r1).

**Function.** Where Ignacio was held up eleven years ago, where rule six brings her back,
and where the story lets her go.
