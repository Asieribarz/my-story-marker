name: The tank, below the orlop
---

**Appearance.** Eight feet by four, lead-lined, filled by bucket from over the side. A
grating over the top, a lamp on a hook above it, and a half-hour glass nailed to the beam
where everyone must pass it.

**Atmosphere.** Warm, close and loud with the sound of the ship's working. The water in it
goes stale in a day and has to be changed, which means opening the grating.

**Function.** The thing Ignacio built for eleven years and cannot stand to look into.
